# Digitalsofts Support Policy

## Support Tiers

### Standard Support
- **Included with**: All product subscriptions
- **Hours**: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (PKT)
- **Channels**: Email, ticketing system
- **Response time**: Within 24 business hours
- **Includes**: Bug fixes, minor configuration changes

### Premium Support
- **Additional cost**: 20% of subscription fee
- **Hours**: Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM (PKT)
- **Channels**: Email, phone, ticketing system, dedicated Slack channel
- **Response time**: Critical — 2 hours, High — 4 hours, Normal — 8 hours
- **Includes**: Priority bug fixes, configuration assistance, quarterly review meetings

### Enterprise Support
- **Custom pricing**
- **Hours**: 24/7/365
- **Channels**: All channels plus dedicated account manager
- **Response time**: Critical — 30 minutes, High — 1 hour, Normal — 4 hours
- **Includes**: Dedicated support engineer, monthly review meetings, proactive monitoring

## Issue Severity Levels

- **Critical**: System is down or data loss occurring. Business operations are halted.
- **High**: Major feature is not working. Significant business impact with no workaround.
- **Medium**: Feature is partially working. Workaround available.
- **Low**: Minor issue, cosmetic defect, or enhancement request.

## Escalation Process

1. Support ticket created by customer
2. Assigned to support engineer within SLA
3. If not resolved within 2x SLA, escalated to senior engineer
4. If not resolved within 4x SLA, escalated to engineering manager
5. Critical issues: direct escalation to CTO
