# Digitalsofts Inventory Management System

## Overview

Digitalsofts Inventory Management is a standalone solution for businesses that need advanced inventory control without a full ERP system. It provides real-time stock visibility across multiple locations, automated reorder management, and comprehensive reporting.

## Key Features

### Multi-Location Management
- Track inventory across warehouses, retail stores, and distribution centers
- Inter-location transfers with approval workflows
- Location-specific pricing and stock rules

### Stock Control
- Real-time stock levels with minimum/maximum thresholds
- Automatic reorder point calculations based on demand history
- Cycle counting and physical inventory support
- Serial number and batch/lot tracking
- Expiry date management with FIFO/FEFO enforcement

### Purchase Management
- Purchase requisition and approval workflow
- Purchase order creation and tracking
- Goods receipt and inspection
- Vendor performance tracking
- Multi-currency purchasing

### Barcode and RFID
- Barcode generation (Code 128, QR codes)
- Mobile barcode scanning (iOS and Android)
- RFID tag support for high-value items
- Label printing integration

### Reporting
- Stock valuation reports (FIFO, LIFO, weighted average)
- Inventory turnover analysis
- Dead stock identification
- Demand forecasting
- Supplier performance reports

## Technical Specifications

- **Deployment**: Cloud (SaaS) or on-premise
- **Mobile**: Native mobile apps for iOS and Android
- **Integration**: API-first design with REST API
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Users**: Scales from 5 to 500 users
