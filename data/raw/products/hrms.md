# Digitalsofts HRMS — Human Resource Management System

## Overview

Digitalsofts HRMS is a complete human resource management solution covering the employee lifecycle from recruitment to retirement. It automates HR processes, ensures compliance, and provides actionable workforce analytics.

## Core Modules

### Employee Management
- Centralized employee database with document management
- Organization structure and reporting hierarchy
- Employee self-service portal
- Digital onboarding workflow

### Attendance and Leave
- Biometric and geolocation-based attendance
- Configurable leave policies and calendars
- Leave request and approval workflow
- Overtime calculation and management

### Payroll
- Multi-company payroll processing
- Tax calculation and compliance (Pakistan tax law)
- Bank file generation for salary disbursement
- Payslip generation and distribution
- Provident fund and EOBI management

### Recruitment
- Job posting and applicant tracking
- Resume parsing and shortlisting
- Interview scheduling and feedback
- Offer letter generation

### Performance Management
- Goal setting and OKR tracking
- 360-degree feedback
- Performance appraisal cycles
- Training needs identification

## Technical Specifications

- **Deployment**: Cloud (SaaS)
- **Mobile**: Employee self-service mobile app (Android, iOS)
- **Integration**: REST API, Active Directory, biometric devices
- **Compliance**: Pakistan labor law compliant
