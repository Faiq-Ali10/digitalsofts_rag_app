# Digitalsofts Demo Request Process

## How to Request a Demo

Customers and prospects can request a product demonstration through the following channels:

1. **Online form**: Visit https://digitalsofts.com/demo and fill out the demo request form
2. **Email**: Send a request to sales@digitalsofts.com
3. **Phone**: Call +92-42-1234-5678 and ask for the sales team
4. **AI Assistant**: Use our enterprise assistant to create a demo request directly

## Information Required

To process a demo request, we need:

- **Contact person name** (required)
- **Email address** (required)
- **Company name** (required)
- **Product of interest** (required)
- **Company size** (number of employees) (optional)
- **Current system/solution** (optional)
- **Specific requirements or use case** (optional)
- **Preferred demo date and time** (optional)

## Demo Process

1. **Request received**: Our sales team reviews the request within 24 hours
2. **Initial contact**: Sales representative contacts the customer to understand requirements
3. **Demo scheduled**: A 60-minute demo session is scheduled at a mutually convenient time
4. **Demo delivered**: Product specialist presents the solution with focus on customer's use case
5. **Follow-up**: Proposal and pricing provided within 48 hours after demo
6. **Trial**: 14-day trial access provided for qualified prospects

## Demo Types

- **Standard demo**: 60-minute overview of core features
- **Deep dive**: 90-minute session focused on specific modules
- **Technical demo**: Architecture, API, and integration walkthrough
- **Custom demo**: Tailored to customer's specific data and workflows

## Notes

- Demos are conducted via Google Meet or Microsoft Teams
- On-site demos available for enterprise prospects in Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad
- All demos are free of charge and without obligation
