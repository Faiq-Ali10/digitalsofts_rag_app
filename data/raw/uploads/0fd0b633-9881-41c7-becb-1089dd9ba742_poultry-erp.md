# Digitalsofts Poultry ERP

## Overview

Digitalsofts Poultry ERP is a comprehensive enterprise resource planning solution specifically designed for poultry farming businesses. It integrates all critical business operations—from hatchery management to sales and distribution—into a single, unified platform.

The system is suitable for small, medium, and large-scale poultry operations including broiler farms, layer farms, breeder farms, and integrated poultry companies.

## Core Modules

### Flock Management
The Flock Management module tracks the complete lifecycle of poultry flocks from placement to processing. Key features include:

- **Flock registration**: Create and manage flock records with breed, placement date, source, and target metrics
- **Daily recording**: Track daily feed consumption, water intake, mortality, body weight, and environmental conditions
- **Growth tracking**: Monitor FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio), ADG (Average Daily Gain), and compare against breed standards
- **Mortality analysis**: Track mortality by cause with alerts for abnormal mortality rates
- **Vaccination scheduling**: Automated vaccination schedule based on breed and farm protocols

### Feed Management
The Feed Management module controls feed inventory, consumption tracking, and cost analysis:

- **Feed inventory**: Track feed stock levels across warehouses and farms
- **Consumption recording**: Daily feed consumption per flock with automatic FCR calculation
- **Feed formulation**: Basic feed formula management with ingredient tracking
- **Cost analysis**: Per-bird and per-kg feed cost calculations
- **Reorder alerts**: Automatic alerts when feed stock falls below minimum levels

### Inventory Management
Comprehensive inventory tracking for all farm supplies:

- **Multi-warehouse support**: Track inventory across multiple locations
- **Stock movements**: Record receipts, issues, transfers, and adjustments
- **Batch tracking**: Lot/batch tracking for medicines, vaccines, and feed
- **Expiry management**: Alerts for items approaching expiry dates
- **Barcode support**: Optional barcode scanning for stock operations

### Sales and Distribution
End-to-end sales process management:

- **Customer management**: Maintain customer records with credit limits and payment terms
- **Sales orders**: Create and process sales orders with automatic pricing
- **Dispatch management**: Plan and track dispatches with route optimization
- **Invoicing**: Automated invoice generation with configurable tax rules
- **Accounts receivable**: Track payments, aging, and outstanding balances

### Reporting and Analytics
Business intelligence and operational reporting:

- **Dashboard**: Real-time operational dashboard with KPIs
- **Flock performance**: Detailed flock performance reports with breed comparisons
- **Financial reports**: P&L, cost analysis, and margin reports
- **Regulatory reporting**: Generate reports required by regulatory authorities
- **Custom reports**: Report builder for creating custom analysis

## Technical Specifications

- **Deployment**: Cloud-hosted (SaaS) or on-premise installation
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **API**: RESTful API for integration
- **Mobile**: Responsive web application with mobile companion app (Android)
- **Users**: Supports up to 500 concurrent users per instance
- **Languages**: English, Urdu
- **Backup**: Automated daily backups with 30-day retention
