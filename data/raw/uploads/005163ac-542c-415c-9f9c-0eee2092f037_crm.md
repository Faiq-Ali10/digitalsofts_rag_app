# Digitalsofts CRM — Customer Relationship Management

## Overview

Digitalsofts CRM helps businesses manage customer relationships, track sales pipelines, and improve customer engagement. It provides a 360-degree view of each customer across all touchpoints.

## Core Features

### Contact and Account Management
- Centralized customer database
- Contact hierarchy and relationships
- Activity timeline and interaction history
- Custom fields and tags

### Sales Pipeline
- Visual pipeline management with drag-and-drop
- Deal tracking with probability scoring
- Automated follow-up reminders
- Sales forecasting and quota tracking
- Win/loss analysis

### Marketing Automation
- Email campaign management
- Lead scoring and nurturing
- Landing page builder
- Campaign ROI tracking

### Customer Support
- Ticket management system
- SLA tracking and escalation
- Knowledge base for self-service
- Customer satisfaction surveys (CSAT, NPS)

### Analytics
- Sales performance dashboards
- Customer segmentation
- Revenue analytics
- Activity and engagement reports

## Technical Specifications

- **Deployment**: Cloud (SaaS) with mobile apps
- **Integration**: Email (Gmail, Outlook), calendar, phone systems
- **API**: RESTful API for custom integrations
- **Security**: Role-based access, audit trails, data encryption

- **Starter**: Up to 5 users — PKR 25,000/month
- **Professional**: Up to 25 users — PKR 75,000/month
- **Enterprise**: Unlimited users — custom pricing
