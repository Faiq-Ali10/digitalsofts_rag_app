# Digitalsofts General ERP Platform

## Overview

Digitalsofts ERP is a modular enterprise resource planning platform designed for medium to large businesses across multiple industries. Built on modern cloud architecture, it provides a unified system for managing core business processes including finance, operations, human resources, and supply chain.

## Core Modules

### Financial Management
- General ledger with multi-currency support
- Accounts payable and receivable
- Fixed asset management
- Budgeting and forecasting
- Tax management and compliance
- Bank reconciliation

### Supply Chain Management
- Procurement and purchase order management
- Vendor management with performance scoring
- Warehouse management with multi-location support
- Demand planning and forecasting
- Logistics and transportation management

### Manufacturing
- Bill of materials (BOM) management
- Production planning and scheduling
- Quality control and inspection
- Work order management
- Shop floor control

### Project Management
- Project planning with Gantt charts
- Resource allocation and capacity planning
- Time and expense tracking
- Project billing and revenue recognition
- Milestone tracking and reporting

### Business Intelligence
- Real-time dashboards and KPIs
- Ad-hoc reporting with drag-and-drop report builder
- Data visualization and charts
- Scheduled report generation
- Export to Excel, PDF, and CSV

## Technical Specifications

- **Architecture**: Microservices-based, cloud-native
- **Deployment**: SaaS (multi-tenant) or dedicated cloud instance
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with read replicas
- **API**: RESTful API and GraphQL
- **Integration**: Pre-built connectors for SAP, Salesforce, QuickBooks
- **Security**: SOC 2 Type II compliant, AES-256 encryption
- **Availability**: 99.9% uptime SLA
