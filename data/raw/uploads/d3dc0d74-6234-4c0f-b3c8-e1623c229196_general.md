# Digitalsofts — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is Digitalsofts?
Digitalsofts is an enterprise software company based in Lahore, Pakistan. We build and deliver business management solutions including ERP, CRM, HRMS, inventory management, and custom software development services. Founded in 2018, we serve over 200 clients across Pakistan and the Middle East.

### What industries does Digitalsofts serve?
We serve multiple industries including:
- Poultry and agriculture
- Manufacturing
- Distribution and logistics
- Retail
- Healthcare
- Education
- Financial services

### Where is Digitalsofts located?
Our headquarters is in Lahore, Pakistan, with offices in Karachi and Islamabad. We also have a representative office in Dubai, UAE.

### Does Digitalsofts offer international services?
Yes, we serve clients internationally, particularly in the GCC region and Southeast Asia. Our products support multi-currency, multi-language, and multi-timezone operations.

## Products

### Which ERP solution is right for my business?
The choice depends on your industry and specific needs:
- **Poultry ERP**: For poultry farming businesses (broiler, layer, breeder operations)
- **General ERP**: For manufacturing, distribution, and multi-industry businesses
- For specialized needs, we offer custom ERP modules

### Can I use individual modules without the full ERP?
Yes, our modular architecture allows you to start with specific modules (e.g., Inventory, HRMS, CRM) and add more as your needs grow. Each module works independently and integrates seamlessly with others.

### Is there a free trial?
We offer a 14-day free trial for all cloud-based products. No credit card required. Contact our sales team or use the demo request form to get started.

### Can Digitalsofts products integrate with my existing systems?
All our products provide RESTful APIs for integration. We have pre-built connectors for popular systems including SAP, Salesforce, QuickBooks, and various banking systems.

## Technical

### What technology stack does Digitalsofts use?
Our products are built using modern technologies:
- **Backend**: Python (FastAPI, Django), Node.js
- **Frontend**: React, Next.js
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Cloud**: AWS, Google Cloud
- **Mobile**: React Native

### Is my data secure?
Yes, we take data security seriously:
- All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- SOC 2 Type II compliance (for cloud products)
- Data centers in AWS regions with multi-AZ redundancy
- Role-based access control and audit trails

### What is the uptime guarantee?
We offer 99.9% uptime SLA for all cloud-hosted products. This excludes scheduled maintenance windows which are communicated 48 hours in advance.

### Can I host the product on my own servers?
Most of our products are available for on-premise deployment. Additional licensing fees apply for on-premise installations. Contact sales for details.
